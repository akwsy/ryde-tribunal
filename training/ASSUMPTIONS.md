# Training assumptions

Owner approval of a ruling does not approve a Rider Advocate, Driver Advocate, or Judge target.
Each ruling and each role target has separate approval metadata. Evidence auditor and fraud
assessor targets are excluded from this pilot and remain on their validated baseline. There are
initially zero eligible labels. The pilot is a small feasibility experiment, not evidence of
production accuracy. No Kaggle account, GPU quota, training run, or candidate improvement is
assumed. Dependency additions are recorded in requirements.txt and require GPU smoke validation.
